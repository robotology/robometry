#include <robometry/CircularBuffer.h>
template<class T>
robometry::CircularBuffer class

Fixed-capacity storage, iterated from oldest to newest.

Contents

  • Reference
    • Public types
    • Constructors, destructors, conversion operators
    • Public functions

Inserting into a full buffer overwrites its oldest element; inserting at capacity zero does nothing. Raw storage is allocated only on construction or capacity changes. Only live elements are constructed and destroyed; unused capacity is left untouched. Elements themselves may allocate memory.

Copies own their elements independently. Structural modifications invalidate iterators. Access from multiple threads requires external synchronization.
